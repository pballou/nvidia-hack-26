{%- if n == 1 %}

Each monthly statement has four parts:

  • Amount due and the due date, at the top of page 1.
  • Usage: the kilowatt-hours (kWh) you used, compared with the same month last year.
  • Charges: delivery, energy supply, and taxes and fees.
  • Payments we received since your last statement.

Your last 12 statements are on the Billing page. Use the download button in a row to save that statement as a PDF.

{%- elif n == 2 %}

Go to Billing and turn on the Autopay switch. We'll pay your full statement balance from {{ ACCOUNT.payment_method }} on each due date, and email you a receipt.

You can turn Autopay off at any time. Changes made before a due date apply to that bill.

{%- elif n == 3 %}

Open the Outage map and select your district on the map. Tell us what's happening and submit. You'll get a reference number right away, and we'll text you when power is back.

Outages our crews are already working on are listed below the map.

{%- elif n == 4 %}

Go to Settings > Accessibility:

  • Text size: choose Small, Medium or Large. The whole portal rescales.
  • High contrast: white text on black with yellow highlights.
  • Reduce motion: turns off movement effects.

Changes apply right away and are saved to your account.

Stay at least 35 feet (10 meters) away and assume the line is live. If anyone is hurt or in danger, call 911. Then call our 24/7 emergency line at {{ EMERGENCY }}.

Don't touch the line or anything it's touching, such as a fence, car or puddle, and never try to move it. If a line falls on your car, stay inside until crews arrive. Please call rather than reporting a downed line online, so we can send a crew immediately.

{%- elif n == 6 %}

Go to Settings > Profile, edit your name, email or phone number, and save your changes.

We use your phone number for call-backs and outage texts, and your email for statements and receipts.
